      Honest limit: a web page cannot connect directly to an Apple Watch, Fitbit or Garmin watch. For them, use option 1 below — sync the watch to its phone app, then enter the numbers here.

      1

      Sync your band to your phone’s health app

      1. Open your band’s app (for example Mi Fitness, NoiseFit, boAt Crest, Fitbit, or Samsung Health) and pair your band.
      2. Android: turn on sharing to Health Connect in the band app’s settings, and allow steps, heart rate, sleep and exercise. iPhone: turn on sharing to Apple Health in the band app’s settings.
      3. Wear the band and let it sync. Then read the numbers from the band app or Health Connect / Apple Health and enter them on the Log a day page.

      2

      Manual entry

      Use the Log a day page. This works for any band, with no account or connection needed.
