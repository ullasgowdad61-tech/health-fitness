      Connect Band

      No band is connected yet. This website does not pull data from any band automatically. Readings are entered from your health band or its app, or imported from a CSV file below.

      Option 1 — Sync your band to your phone’s health app

      1. Open your band’s app (for example Mi Fitness, NoiseFit, boAt Crest, Fitbit, or Samsung Health) and pair your band.
      2. Android: turn on sharing to Health Connect in the band app’s settings, and allow steps, heart rate, sleep and exercise. iPhone: turn on sharing to Apple Health in the band app’s settings.
      3. Wear the band and let it sync. Then read the numbers from the band app or Health Connect / Apple Health and enter them in “Log a day’s band readings” above, or export a CSV and import it below.

      Option 2 — Manual entry

      Use the “Log a day’s band readings” form. This works for any band, with no account or connection needed.

      Option 3 — CSV import

      CSV columns, in this order: date,steps,heart_rate,sleep_hours,calories,distance_km,weight_kg
      Example: 2026-11-24,8500,72,7.5,2100,6.2,68.5 — empty cells are allowed. Importing a date that already exists updates that day’s reading.

      Goals

      Defaults shown are 10,000 steps a day and 3 workouts a week. They are editable placeholders, not recorded data — change them and save to make them yours.

      All data is stored only in this browser (localStorage). “Delete all my data” clears readings, workouts and saved goals on this device.
